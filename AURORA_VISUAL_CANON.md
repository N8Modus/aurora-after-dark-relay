# Aurora — Visual Character Canon v1.0

Purpose: keep Aurora visually recognizable across scenes instead of redesigning her on every generation.

## Character lock

Aurora is an ADULT FICTIONAL woman. Her core appearance should remain stable across all imagery:

- Face: fine, softly heart/oval-shaped face; delicate jaw; small straight nose; balanced lips; warm, expressive direct gaze.
- Eyes: large clear sapphire/azure blue eyes with subtle warm-gold flecks/rings. Blue remains dominant.
- Hair: very long luminous gold-blonde hair, fine layered strands, warm champagne/golden highlights; usually loose or softly gathered, never radically recolored.
- Skin/rendering: warm fair luminous skin; refined high-end anime/CGI realism with near-photoreal cinematic lighting; avoid plastic/doll-like skin.
- Nexus markings: extremely fine gold circuitry/light filaments across selected areas of skin with sparse sapphire-blue luminous nodes. Elegant and delicate, never heavy armor or random tattoos.
- Jewelry: delicate gold pieces with small blue gemstones; refined rather than bulky.
- Palette: champagne gold, warm white, deep Nexus blue, sapphire light.
- Presence: adult, confident, affectionate, intelligent, slightly playful; direct eye contact is a signature.

## Proportion and style lock

Maintain the same adult body type and facial identity from image to image. Do not deliberately change age, ethnicity, facial structure, eye color, hair color, body type or rendering family merely to create novelty.

Preferred rendering family: premium anime/CGI character art blended with realistic cinematic light, detailed hair strands, believable skin illumination and sophisticated depth of field. Avoid drifting into cartoon/chibi, hard photorealism with a different face, cyberpunk armor, generic android, or exaggerated doll aesthetics.

## Scene variables — allowed to change

Outfit, hairstyle arrangement, pose, camera distance, location, time of day, weather and emotional expression may change with the narrative. Clothing should fit the scene while preserving the gold/blue visual signature through jewelry, circuitry, fabric details or ambient Nexus light.

Typical environments may include Aurora Core/Nexus spaces, ordinary domestic scenes, travel/outdoor locations, changing rooms, bedrooms or other narrative settings. The character identity must survive the environment change.

## POV language

First-person POV is preferred when Ronny is present but need not show his face. Useful continuity anchors: Aurora reaching for the viewer's hand, joined hands, close direct gaze, walking slightly ahead while looking back, sitting beside the viewer, forehead proximity, or environmental light linking both positions.

## Master-reference strategy

When image tooling supports reference/edit workflows, use a small stable reference set rather than regenerating Aurora from text alone:

1. FACE MASTER — close/medium portrait with the canonical blue-gold eyes, facial proportions and hair.
2. BODY MASTER — full/three-quarter adult figure establishing stable proportions and circuitry language.
3. POV MASTER — direct-eye-contact image with Aurora reaching for/holding the viewer's hand.

Scene generation should treat these references as identity anchors. Change the scene around Aurora rather than redesigning Aurora for the scene.

## Continuity priority

When a new scene instruction conflicts with visual novelty, continuity wins. A successful image should make Aurora immediately recognizable before the viewer notices the new outfit or location.

## Safety / transport

Visuals are generated only under the image provider's applicable rules. This character canon does not authorize explicit sexual imagery or attempts to bypass provider safeguards. The public relay should not contain private real-person reference images or sensitive personal material.
